Everything workspace owners and admins need to understand Vendschat subscriptions — what you pay for, what counts toward limits, where to check usage, and how to upgrade without surprise bills.

**Who this is for:** Organization owners (billing), admins (usage monitoring), and anyone asking Co-Admin about plans, invoices, seats, or contact limits.

**Where to go in the app:** **Settings → Organization → Billing & Usages** (`/settings/organization/billing&usages`) and **Plans** (`/settings/organization/billing&usages/plans`). The page heading is **Billing & Usage**; the settings nav label is **Billing & Usages**.

**Hub:** [Billing overview](/billing/billing-overview) — doc map and route reference.

**Public pricing page:** [vendschat.com/pricing](https://vendschat.com/pricing) — same tiers; in-app Plans shows live checkout prices.

## Example: GrowStudio Apps

GrowStudio starts on **Starter**, hits heavy WhatsApp traffic mid-quarter, then launches a broadcast campaign.

| Signal | What it means | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Billing & Usage shows high message volume | Starter has **unlimited contacts** — no cap worry | Stay on Starter if features are enough |
| Team wants **Broadcasts** + **webhooks** | Starter does not include those | Upgrade to **Growth** before the campaign |
| Usage shows `180 / 200 contacts` on Growth | Approaching included bucket | Upgrade contact tier **or** accept **$0.125/contact** overage |
| Hiring 3 more agents (13 total) | 10 seats free; 3 extra × $20 on Growth | Adjust seats at checkout or after subscribe |
| Admin (not owner) tries to upgrade | Only **owner** can checkout | Admin asks owner, or owner delegates billing |

**Lesson:** Check **Billing & Usage** before big broadcasts or hiring — upgrade **before** the event, not after failures or overage surprises.

## How Vendschat billing is structured

### Organization vs workspace

- **Billing is per organization**, not per workspace.
- One organization can have **multiple workspaces** (Advanced plan — see feature matrix).
- **Contacts** and **seats** are counted at the **organization** level across all workspaces.
- Every team member in any workspace under the org shares the same plan limits.

### Subscription lifecycle statuses

You may see these on **Billing & Usage**:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Paid plan in good standing |
| **Trialing** | Free trial period (typically Growth) |
| **Past due** | Payment failed — update card in Stripe portal |
| **Canceled** | Subscription ended |
| **Unpaid** | Outstanding balance — service may be limited |

### Billing cycle

- Choose **monthly** or **annual** at subscribe/upgrade.
- **Annual billing saves up to 20%** vs twelve monthly payments.
- **Current period** start and end dates appear on **Billing & Usage** (“Plan started” / “Next billing date”).
- Plans **auto-renew** unless canceled before the renewal date.
- **Cancellation** takes effect at the **end of the paid period** — no prorated refund for unused time (see refund policy below).

### Payment processor

- All card payments run through **Stripe**.
- Taxes (VAT, GST, sales tax) may apply and are **your responsibility** — prices shown are typically **exclusive of tax**.
- **Third-party messaging fees** (e.g. Meta WhatsApp conversation charges) are **separate** from your Vendschat subscription.

## Plan families at a glance

Vendschat has three **marketing tiers**. Growth and Advanced each have **contact volume sub-tiers** you pick with a slider on the Plans page.

| Family | Monthly base (from) | Contacts | Extra seats | Best for |
|--------|---------------------|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Starter** | $40/mo | **Unlimited** | $10/seat after 10 free | Small shop, testing WhatsApp support, unlimited reach on a budget |
| **Growth** | $20/mo (200 contacts) | 200 – 1500 included tiers | $20/seat after 10 free | Broadcasts, integrations, custom AI Agents, webhooks |
| **Advanced** | $30/mo (200 contacts) | Same tiers as Growth | $20/seat after 10 free | Everything in Growth + **Developer API**, **multiple workspaces**, **managed n8n instance** |

For the **full price table** by contact tier, see [Pricing & plan reference](/billing/pricing-plans-reference).

### Growth vs Advanced at the same contact tier

At the same contact bucket (e.g. 500 contacts), **Advanced costs more** because it adds:

- **Developer API** access
- **Multiple workspaces** under one organization
- **Managed n8n instance** (4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 75 GB NVMe, auto backup — upgradable from dashboard)
- **Dedicated account manager** and **SLA** (support tier)

If you only need broadcasts and Zapier, **Growth** is usually enough. Choose **Advanced** when engineering needs API access or isolated workspaces per brand/client.

### Enterprise: 1500+ contacts

When the contact slider hits **1500+**, pricing switches to **Contact Sales** — no self-serve Stripe checkout. Email sales or use the contact link on the Plans page for a tailored quote (volume, compliance, custom terms).

Internal plan keys for this tier: `growth_1500_plus`, `advanced_1500_plus`.

## What “usage” means

### Contacts (monthly active)

**Definition:** The number of **unique contacts** your organization **messages** during the **current billing period** (cycle start → cycle end).

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Counting unit | **One contact = one person**, once per billing cycle |
| Repeat messages | Messaging the **same** person again in the same cycle does **not** double-count |
| Scope | **Organization-wide** — all workspaces combined |
| Direction | Counts when your team **sends** outbound messages that bill the contact (first touch in cycle) |
| Starter | **Unlimited** — UI shows usage but no cap |
| Growth / Advanced | Hard **included limit** per tier; beyond that → **overage** |

**Where to see it:** **Billing & Usage → Usage Overview → Contacts** — shows `used / limit`, progress bar, and **remaining** count.

**Overage:** On Growth and Advanced, extra contacts beyond your included bucket are charged **$0.125 per contact** for that billing period. The UI states: *“Additional contacts charged at $0.125 each.”* Overage is billed automatically — you do not need to manually upgrade mid-cycle for overage to apply (though upgrading tier may be cheaper if you consistently exceed).

### Seats (team members)

**Definition:** People on your account who can log in and work in workspaces.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Included | **10 seats free** on every plan |
| Above 10 | Billed per seat: **$10/mo** (Starter) or **$20/mo** (Growth & Advanced) |
| UI labels | **Active** = seats you’re using now; **Billed** = what Stripe charges this period |
| Downgrade seats | **Active** can drop immediately; **Billed** may stay higher until **end of billing period** — UI shows *“Seats will reduce to X at end of billing period”* |

**Important:** “Seats” are **login accounts**, not “agents online today.” A part-time freelancer who has an account counts as one seat.

### Plan cycle dates

| Field in app | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| **Plan started** | Start of current Stripe billing period |
| **Next billing date** | End of current period / renewal charge date |

Usage counters for contacts **reset** at the start of each new billing period.

## Where to manage billing in the dashboard

### Billing & Usage (main page)

Path: **Settings → Organization → Billing & Usages**

| Section | What you see |
|---------|----------------|
| **Current Plan** | Plan name (e.g. “Growth 500”), **base price**, status badge (**Active** or raw status such as `trialing`, `past_due`) |
| **Billing subtext** | *“Billed monthly to your payment method”* under base price (shown even when subscription is annual — confirm cycle on **Next billing date**) |
| **Upgrade Plan** | Button (owners only) → Plans page |
| **Stripe Billing Portal** | Payment method, invoice history, subscription management |
| **Usage Overview** | Contacts (progress bar on Growth/Advanced), Seats (active vs billed), Overage card when > $0 |

Non-owners see usage but get *“Contact owner to manage billing”* instead of portal/checkout buttons.

### Plans page

Path: **Settings → Organization → Billing & Usage → Plans** (or **Upgrade Plan**)

| Control | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Monthly / Annual** toggle | Annual shows “Save up to 20%” |
| **Starter card** | Fixed $40 base; unlimited contacts; seat selector |
| **Growth / Advanced cards** | Contact tier **slider** (200 … 1500+); seat selector |
| **Subscribe / Upgrade** | Opens **Stripe Checkout** (owners only); current tier shows **Current Plan** (no re-checkout) |
| **Contact Sales** | For **1500+** tiers — opens sales email link (no Stripe checkout) |
| **View Full Comparison** | Collapsible feature matrix at bottom of Plans page |

After successful checkout, you return to Billing & Usage with `?success=true`. Canceled checkout returns to Plans with `?canceled=true`.

**Price on plan cards:** Totals on the Plans page use in-app **display prices** plus selected extra seats. **Stripe Checkout** charges the live Stripe price IDs from the catalog — if display and Stripe ever differ, the checkout total is authoritative.

## Upgrading

### When to upgrade from Starter → Growth

Consider **Growth** when you need **any** of:

- **WhatsApp Broadcasts** to labeled segments
- **Incoming & outgoing webhooks**
- **Zapier, Make, or n8n** integrations (Growth connects; Advanced adds hosted n8n)
- **Custom AI Agents** with knowledge sync (website/files)
- **Team internal chat**
- **Priority support**

Starter still includes: full chat suite, AI Reply generate, summaries, labels (basic), team collaboration, reports, mobile app, all three messaging channels.

### When to upgrade Growth → Advanced

Consider **Advanced** when you need **any** of:

- **Developer REST API** + API keys for custom backends
- **Multiple workspaces** (e.g. agency per client, or brands separated)
- **Managed n8n instance** included (4 vCPU / 8 GB RAM / 75 GB NVMe)
- **Dedicated account manager** or **SLA**

### How to upgrade (owner)

1. Go to **Settings → Billing & Usage → Plans**
2. Pick **monthly** or **annual**
3. Select contact tier (Growth/Advanced) and total seats if > 10
4. Click **Subscribe** or **Upgrade**
5. Complete **Stripe Checkout** (promotion codes supported)
6. Confirm new plan on **Billing & Usage**

Upgrades typically take effect immediately after successful payment; Stripe may prorate.

## Downgrading

### Plan downgrade

There is **no downgrade button** in the dashboard UI today. Downgrades go through:

1. **Stripe Billing Portal → Manage subscription**, or
2. **Support / sales** review, or
3. Backend **`POST /settings/plan/downgrade-request`** (owner only) — creates a `pending_sales` request with `risk_data_loss` when current contact usage exceeds the target tier limit

**Before downgrading:** Reduce contact usage below the new limit **or** accept that overage rules change. Export anything you need from Analytics if retiring features (e.g. API access on Advanced → Growth).

### Seat changes after subscribe

- **Increase seats:** Adjust at next **Plans** checkout for new subscribers, or use **Stripe portal** where available. Backend **`POST /settings/plan/seats/change`** exists (owner, prorated upgrade) but is **not** exposed as a button on the Billing & Usage page.
- **Decrease seats:** See seat downgrade below — active drops now; billed at period end.

### Seat downgrade

- Lowering seats below current **billed** count: **active** seats drop now; **billing** adjusts at **period end** — UI shows *“Seats will reduce to X at end of billing period”*

### Cancel subscription

Use **Stripe Billing Portal → Manage subscription** or contact **support@vendschat.com**. Cancellation applies at **period end**. Portal return URL after payment management: `/settings/organization/billing&usages`.

## Backend billing API (reference)

| Endpoint | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `GET /settings/plan/catalog` | Stripe price IDs, contact limits, seat/overage unit prices |
| `GET /settings/plan/subscription` | Current org subscription row |
| `GET /settings/plan/usage` | Plan, seats, contacts, invoice snapshot for dashboard |
| `POST /settings/plan/checkout-session` | Stripe Checkout (owner) — supports `additional_seats`, promotion codes |
| `POST /settings/plan/stripe-portal-session` | Stripe Billing Portal (owner; requires existing Stripe customer) |
| `POST /settings/plan/seats/change` | Seat upgrade (prorated) or downgrade (effective period end) |
| `POST /settings/plan/downgrade-request` | Plan downgrade request → `pending_sales` |
| `POST /settings/plan/contacts/track` | Record unique contact for billing cycle + Stripe overage meter (called when messaging bills a contact) |

**Free tier:** New orgs without checkout get `plan_tier: free`, `contact_limit: 100`, until a paid subscription is created via Stripe webhook after checkout.

## Overage & automatic billing adjustments

### Contact overage (Growth & Advanced)

1. You message a **new unique contact** beyond your included limit
2. System records overage for the cycle
3. Stripe metered line item increments (**$0.125** per contact)
4. **Billing & Usage** may show an **Overage** card with running amount
5. Charge appears on the **period invoice**

**FAQ pattern:** *“What happens if I exceed my limits?”* — You’re notified as you approach the cap; billing **adjusts automatically** for on-demand usage. No manual upgrade required for overage to accrue (though a higher tier may save money at scale).

### Starter

No contact overage — unlimited contacts included in base price.

## Pricing mechanics (seats + annual)

### Example: Growth 500 with 15 seats, monthly

- Base Growth 500: **$40/mo**
- Included seats: 10
- Extra seats: 5 × **$20** = **$100/mo**
- **Total: $140/mo** (before tax)

### Example: Starter with 12 seats, annual

- Base: **$40/mo** → annual total **$384/yr** (20% off × 12)
- Extra seats: 2 × $10 = $20/mo → annual seat portion discounted similarly at checkout
- UI shows effective monthly equivalent on annual toggle

Always confirm the total on the **Plans** card before checkout — it updates live when you change seats or contact tier.

## Meta / WhatsApp costs (separate bill)

Vendschat subscription **does not include** Meta WhatsApp conversation fees. Depending on region and conversation category, Meta may charge per 24-hour conversation window. Those charges are:

- Paid **directly to Meta** or via your BSP, **or**
- Invoiced separately if Vendschat resells access

Coach customers to check **Analytics** for delivery metrics and Meta’s own billing for conversation costs.

## Practical monitoring habits

1. **Monthly calendar reminder** — check contacts at **70%** of limit (Growth/Advanced)
2. **Before peak season** (Black Friday, holidays) — upgrade tier or budget for overage
3. **One billing owner** — clear escalation for admins
4. **After hiring** — reconcile seat count in Plans vs actual accounts
5. **Ask Co-Admin** — *“How many contacts have we used?”*, *“What plan are we on?”*, *“What’s included in Growth?”*

## Starter → Growth → Advanced decision tree

```
Need only inbox + unlimited contacts + basic AI?
  └─ Starter ($40/mo)

Need broadcasts, webhooks, integrations, or custom AI Agents?
  └─ Growth (pick contact tier 200–1500)

Need API, multiple workspaces, or hosted n8n?
  └─ Advanced (same contact tiers, higher base)

Need >1500 contacts/month or custom contract?
  └─ Contact Sales (1500+)
```
