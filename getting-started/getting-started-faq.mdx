## Account & sign-up

### How do I create a Vendschat account?

Go to **`/signup`** or use **Start trial** from [vendschat.com](https://vendschat.com).

Choose:
- **Continue with Google**, or  
- **Email** — full name, company name, work email, password  

### What’s the difference between email signup and Google signup?

| | **Email signup** | **Google signup (new user)** |
|---|------------------|------------------------------|
| Org + workspace | Created **immediately** at signup | Created on **`/oauth-onboarding`** after Google auth |
| Lands on | `/chat` | `/oauth-onboarding` → then `/chat` |
| Company name | From **Company name** field | From **Organization name** on oauth-onboarding |
| Default workspace | **Headquarters** | **Headquarters** (if workspace name left blank) |

### Do I need a credit card to sign up?

The sign-up page says **“No credit card required”** for the trial. Upgrade and payment happen later in **Settings → Organization → Billing & Usage**. See [billing docs](/billing/plans-and-usage) for trial length and plan details.

### What password rules apply?

For email accounts: uppercase, lowercase, number, special character, minimum 8 characters. The form shows a live checklist and strength meter.

### I signed up with Google and see “Set up your workspace” — is that normal?

**Yes.** That’s **`/oauth-onboarding`**. Enter your **organization name** (required) and optional **workspace name**, then **Continue**. You only see this once for a new Google account.

### I already have an account but Google sends me to oauth-onboarding

You should redirect to **Chat** automatically. If not, sign out and sign in with the account that already has a workspace, or complete the form if you truly have no workspace yet.

### Where do I sign in?

**`/signin`** — email/password or **Continue with Google**.

## Onboarding page vs oauth-onboarding

### What is the Onboarding page?

**`/onboarding`** — in-app checklist: *“Let's get you set up”* with four steps:

1. Connect a channel  
2. Create labels  
3. Set up integrations / automation  
4. Invite team  

Open from sidebar **Onboarding** or mobile **More → Onboarding**.

### What is oauth-onboarding?

**`/oauth-onboarding`** — one-time **account** setup for new **Google** users without a workspace. Not the same as the product Onboarding checklist.

### Where do I go after email signup — onboarding or chat?

**Chat** (`/chat`). Your organization and workspace already exist. Optionally visit **`/onboarding`** for the setup checklist.

### Can I skip the Onboarding checklist?

**Yes.** Nothing blocks **Chat** if you skip it. The page is guidance — connect channels and invite team when you’re ready.

### I don’t see “Onboarding” in the sidebar

Likely **Member (Chat-only)** role — nav may hide setup pages. Owners/admins see it. You can still open **`/onboarding`** directly if authenticated.

## Onboarding steps (detailed)

### Step 1 — Where do I connect WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram?

**Onboarding → Manage channels** → `/settings/workspace/channels` → **Add Channel** → catalog.

Or: **Settings → Workspace → Channels**.

Full guide: [channels overview](/channels/workspaces-and-channels-overview).

### Is there a video for channel setup?

**Yes** — embedded in Onboarding Step 1 accordion (*“How it works”*).

### Step 2 — Where do I create labels?

**Onboarding → Create Your First Label** → `/settings/workspace/labels`.

Or: **Settings → Workspace → Labels**.

### How many labels should I create at first?

At least **two** meaningful tags (e.g. `Sales`, `Support`) before high message volume.

### Step 3 — What does “Create AI Agent” mean?

Opens **AI Agents** (`/ai-agents`) — browse templates or create from scratch. Test before you assign live threads.

Integrations stay at **Settings → Workspace → Integrations** (`/settings/workspace/integrations`) — Agent Skill, Lovable, MCP, n8n, Zapier, Make, HubSpot.

Optional for day one.

### Step 4 — How do I invite teammates?

**Onboarding → Add Team Member** → `/settings/workspace/team-members`.

Or: **Settings → Workspace → Team Members → Invite**.

## Invited teammates

### I got an invite email — what do I do?

Click the link → accept page → set password (or use existing) → **Accept Invitation** → **Chat**.

You join the inviter’s workspace — no separate company signup.

### Do invited users see the Onboarding page?

They **can** if their role includes **overview** access. Many **Members** only see **Chat**.

### What role should I give a support agent?

**Member** — Chat-only. **Admin** if they manage channels and templates.

## First channel & Chat

### I connected a channel but Chat is empty

Customers must **send you a message first** on that channel (inbound). Or your team starts outbound where the channel allows it.

### How do I know my channel is ready?

**Settings → Workspace → Channels** — status should be **active** (not pending/disconnected).

### Support only vs Support, sales & ads for WhatsApp?

**Support only** — inbox + templates. **Support, sales & ads** — adds Click-to-WhatsApp ads. See [WhatsApp doc](/channels/whatsapp).

### Where is my first reply?

**Chat** → select conversation → type in composer → send.

Walkthrough: [First channel & inbox](/getting-started/first-channel-and-inbox).

## Roles & access during setup

### Who can connect channels?

Usually **Owner** or **Admin** with Settings access.

### Who pays and upgrades the plan?

**Organization owner** — **Settings → Organization → Billing & Usage**.

### Can members complete onboarding steps?

Only if they can reach **Settings** pages. Members invited for Chat often cannot connect channels themselves.

## Help & resources

### What are the Resources on the Onboarding page?

Right sidebar: **Intro**, **Contact**, **Book**, **Help center**, **Video guides** — shortcuts to learning and support content.

### How do I ask questions in the app?

Use **AI Workspace Co-Admin** — setup how-tos and live workspace status.

### Should I turn on AI Agents on day one?

Usually **no**. Handle manual replies first; add **AI Agents** when the same questions repeat. See [AI Agents overview](/ai-agents/ai-agents-overview).

### What’s the difference between Co-Admin and AI Agents?

| | **Co-Admin** | **AI Agents** |
|---|--------------|---------------|
| Helps | **Your team** | **Your customers** |
| Where | Co-Admin chat | Assigned threads in **Chat** |
