## How you get started (two paths)

### Path A — Email sign-up

1. **`/signup`** — name, company, email, password  
2. Land on **Chat** (org + **Headquarters** workspace created for you)  
3. Open **Onboarding** → connect channel, labels, invite team  

### Path B — Google sign-up

1. **`/signup`** or **`/signin`** → **Continue with Google**  
2. New user: **`/oauth-onboarding`** — organization + workspace name  
3. Land on **Chat**  
4. Open **Onboarding** for the product checklist  

**Invited by a teammate?** Accept email invite → **Chat** — skip both paths above.

Full detail: [Onboarding & setup guide](/getting-started/onboarding-and-setup-guide).

## Example: A week with Luna Home Decor

Luna sells furniture on Instagram and WhatsApp. Here’s how a small team uses Vendschat:

| Day | What happens |
|-----|----------------|
| **Monday** | Owner completes **Onboarding** Step 1 — connects WhatsApp and Instagram (**Settings → Workspace → Channels**). Creates labels `Orders`, `Delivery`, `VIP` (Step 2). |
| **Tuesday** | Two agents invited with **Chat-only** access (Step 4). First customer DMs about a sofa — appears in **Chat**. |
| **Wednesday** | They train a **Support AI Agent** on their FAQ page. Assign it to night shift threads. |
| **Thursday** | Flash sale: **Broadcast** to everyone labeled `Newsletter` with an approved template. |
| **Friday** | Manager checks **Analytics** — read rate on the sale template was 68%. Co-Admin asked: *“How many open threads?”* |

No coding. No switching between phone apps. One place for the whole team.

## Your first 30 minutes

| # | Action | Where |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | Finish account (signup or oauth-onboarding) | `/signup` or `/oauth-onboarding` |
| 2 | **Onboarding** Step 1 — connect one channel | `/onboarding` → **Manage channels** |
| 3 | **Onboarding** Step 2 — create one label | **Create Your First Label** |
| 4 | Open **Chat** — reply when a message arrives | `/chat` |
| 5 | **Onboarding** Step 4 — invite one teammate | **Add Team Member** |
| 6 | Ask **Co-Admin** — *“How do I connect WhatsApp?”* | Co-Admin chat |

Start small. Add AI, broadcasts, and automations when the basics feel smooth.

## Onboarding checklist (at a glance)

The **`/onboarding`** page title: *“Let's get you set up”*

| Step | Title | Button goes to |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Connect a communication channel | `/settings/workspace/channels` |
| 2 | Create labels for chat | `/settings/workspace/labels` |
| 3 | Create AI Agent | `/ai-agents` |
| 4 | Invite your team | `/settings/workspace/team-members` |

Step 3 opens **AI Agents**. Integrations stay at **Settings → Workspace → Integrations** (`/settings/workspace/integrations`) — Agent Skill, Lovable, MCP, Zapier, Make, n8n, HubSpot.
