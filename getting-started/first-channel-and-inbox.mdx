Get from zero to your first team reply in one sitting — whether you arrived via **email signup**, **Google oauth-onboarding**, or the in-app **Onboarding** checklist.

**Prerequisite:** You have a workspace (you’re past `/oauth-onboarding` if you used Google).  
**Main hub:** [Onboarding & setup guide](/getting-started/onboarding-and-setup-guide)

---

## Quick reference

| Step | Action | Path |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Connect channel | **Onboarding** Step 1 → **Manage channels** → **Add Channel** |
| 2 | Create labels | **Onboarding** Step 2 → **Create Your First Label** |
| 3 | First reply | **Chat** → conversation → composer |
| 4 | Invite teammate | **Onboarding** Step 4 → **Add Team Member** |

---

## Example: FreshBite Meal Prep

**FreshBite** delivers healthy meals in Dhaka. Support lead **Anika** has never used a shared inbox before. Her goal: stop answering WhatsApp on a single phone.

### Before you start — open Onboarding (optional but recommended)

**Sidebar → Onboarding** (`/onboarding`) — Step 1 is expanded by default.

---

### Step 1 — Connect WhatsApp

**From Onboarding:** Click **Manage channels** → `/settings/workspace/channels`  
**Or:** **Settings → Workspace → Channels → Add Channel**

1. **Channel Catalog** → **WhatsApp Business** → **Connect Channel**  
2. **Choose use case:**  
   - **Support only** — inbox + templates only  
   - **Support, sales & ads** — also Click-to-WhatsApp ads  
3. **Prepare** — check authorization box → **Continue**  
4. **Connect with Facebook** — complete Meta embedded signup  
5. Wait until status is **Active** on the Channels list  

**URLs:**

| Page | Path |
|------|------|
| Channels list | `/settings/workspace/channels` |
| Catalog | `/settings/workspace/channels/catalog` |
| WhatsApp setup | `/settings/workspace/channels/whatsapp/setup` |

**Tip:** Step 1 on the Onboarding page includes a **video guide** under *“How it works”*.

Deep dive: [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp)

---

### Step 2 — Create labels before volume hits

**From Onboarding:** **Create Your First Label** → `/settings/workspace/labels`  
**Or:** **Settings → Workspace → Labels**

Anika creates three labels:

| Label | Why |
|-------|-----|
| `New order` | Routing to sales |
| `Change address` | Ops team |
| `Complaint` | Manager review |

When a message says “wrong address,” she tags it instantly — no scrolling through mixed threads.

Deep dive: [Shared inbox & labels](/inbox/shared-inbox-and-labels)

---

### Step 3 — First reply in Chat

1. Open **Chat** from the sidebar → `/chat`  
2. Click the new conversation (appears when a customer messages your connected number)  
3. Type a reply in the composer and send  

The customer sees the message on WhatsApp. Anika’s colleague **Rafi** sees the same thread on his laptop — that’s the shared inbox.

**No conversations yet?** A customer must message your connected number first — or your team starts outbound where the channel allows it.

---

### Step 4 — Invite Rafi

**From Onboarding:** **Add Team Member** → `/settings/workspace/team-members`  
**Or:** **Settings → Workspace → Team Members → Invite**

| Role | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Member (Chat-only)** | Rafi should only reply, not change billing or channels |
| **Admin** | Rafi will manage channels and templates |

Rafi accepts the email invite and lands in **Chat**.

---

### Step 5 (optional) — Skim automation

**Onboarding** Step 3 → **Set Up Integrations** → `/settings/workspace/integrations`

Not required on day one. Come back when you want Zapier, n8n, or **AI Agents**.

## What good looks like after day one

- ✅ At least one channel **Active**  
- ✅ Two or more labels your team actually uses  
- ✅ Two teammates who’ve both replied from **Chat**  
- ✅ Unread badges going down during the shift

## Common first-day questions

**“I don’t see any conversations yet.”**  
A customer must message your connected number first — or your team starts an outbound conversation where the channel allows it.

**“I’m stuck on Google workspace setup.”**  
That’s `/oauth-onboarding` — complete organization name, then you’ll reach **Chat**. See [Onboarding guide](/getting-started/onboarding-and-setup-guide).

**“Where is the onboarding checklist?”**  
**Onboarding** in the sidebar → `/onboarding` — four accordion steps.

**“Can we both reply at once?”**  
Yes. Assign threads so ownership is clear, or use labels to show who’s handling what.

**“Why can’t I type a free WhatsApp message?”**  
Outside the 24-hour customer window you need an approved **template**. See [WhatsApp doc](/channels/whatsapp).

**“Should we turn on AI today?”**  
Usually not on day one. Get comfortable with manual replies, then add an **AI Agent** when the same questions repeat.

**“Where do I get help?”**  
Open **AI Workspace Co-Admin** and ask in plain language — or use the **Resources** panel on the Onboarding page (Help center, Video guides).
