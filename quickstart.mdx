Finish these steps before you train an AI Agent or send a broadcast.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Email path: `/signup` — name, company, email, password. You land on **Chat** with a Headquarters workspace.

    Google path: **Continue with Google**. New users complete `/oauth-onboarding` (organization + workspace name).

    Invited by a teammate? Accept the email invite and skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect one channel">
    Open **Onboarding** → Step 1, or **Settings → Workspace → Channels**. Connect WhatsApp (Support only unless you run ads). Wait until the channel is **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create one label">
    **Settings → Workspace → Labels**. Example: `Orders`. Add keywords if you want auto-apply in Chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply in Chat">
    Open **Chat**. When a message arrives, reply from the composer. Assign the thread if you work as a team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite one teammate">
    Onboarding Step 4 or **Settings → Workspace → Team**. **Chat-only** is enough for front desk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Day-one bar: one **Active** channel, two labels the team actually uses, two people who have replied from Chat.
</Check>

## What not to do yet

- Do not submit a WhatsApp template to Meta until you know the first message you will send.
- Do not turn a customer **AI Agent** live without the [sandbox test](/ai-agents/knowledge-sources-and-testing).
- Do not send a **Broadcast** without a Meta-**Approved** template and a dry-run to an internal number.
