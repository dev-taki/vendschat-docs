**Scenario:** **Metro Clinics** runs three locations on one shared WhatsApp number. Patients message for appointments, results, and insurance questions — peak volume Monday mornings.

**Plan:** **Starter** initially (unlimited contacts); **Growth** when they add reminder **broadcasts** and a custom **AI Agent**.

---

## The challenge

- Receptionists overwhelmed with *“Is my appointment today?”* and *“Are my labs ready?”*
- No-shows cost money — reminders must go out reliably
- After-hours messages pile up until Tuesday
- Must not give medical advice in automated replies

---

## How Metro Clinics uses Vendschat

### One WhatsApp number, whole team

**Settings → Workspace → Channels** → WhatsApp **Support only** use case (no paid ads).

All three locations share one inbox in **Chat** — agents use **labels** for location and visit type, not separate numbers.

| Label | Use |
|-------|-----|
| `Booking` | New appointment requests |
| `Results` | Lab/imaging questions |
| `Insurance` | Coverage / referral |
| `Location-North` | Routed to north desk |

Keywords: `appointment`, `book` → `Booking`; `lab`, `results` → `Results`.

### AI Agent — receptionist, not doctor

| Setting | Value |
|---------|--------|
| **Agent** | Metro Reception |
| **Template** | Receptionist |
| **Knowledge** | Hours PDF, parking, accepted insurance list (no clinical content) |
| **Instructions** | Never diagnose; never interpret results; offer to connect to staff |
| **Hours** | **Active** after 6 PM and weekends; **Paused** when front desk is staffed |

Assign agent to **unassigned** threads only — staff assign themselves when they take over.

**Compliance:** Bot handles logistics only. Clinical questions get: *“A team member will follow up during business hours.”*

### Templates for reminders

Approved WhatsApp templates in **Templates**:

| Template | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `appointment_reminder` | Date + time variables + **Confirm** button |
| `results_ready` | Pickup instructions (no result values in message) |

Send single reminders from **Chat** composer when within session window; use templates when 24h window expired.

### Broadcast reminders (Growth)

Sunday evening batch for Monday appointments:

1. During booking chats, staff label threads `Appt-Mon`
2. **Broadcast → New broadcast** → Existing contacts → label `Appt-Mon` → template `appointment_reminder`
3. Schedule Sunday 5 PM UTC
4. Monday AM: filter **Chat** for **Confirm** replies and `Booking` overflow

### Team setup

| Role | Vendschat access |
|------|------------------|
| Front desk (3) | **Member (Chat-only)** |
| Office manager | **Admin** — Labels, Templates, Channels |
| Practice owner | **Owner** — billing |

**Groups:** `Front Desk`, `Billing` — assign threads in **Assigned to**.

### Messenger & Instagram (optional)

Metro adds **Instagram** for marketing but keeps **WhatsApp** as the primary service line. Instagram DMs get label `Social-lead` — converted to WhatsApp for booking when possible.

### Analytics

Office manager checks **Analytics** → WhatsApp channel weekly:

- **Failed** on reminder template? Fix variable mapping or patient numbers
- **Read rate** on reminders — adjust send time (Sunday vs Monday early)

### Co-Admin

Reception lead: *“How many contacts have we used this month?”* → billing usage (organization level). *“Delivery rate last week?”* → channel analytics.

## Week-one timeline

| Day | Task |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Connect WhatsApp, confirm **active** status |
| 2 | Labels + keywords |
| 3 | Templates submitted to Meta (wait for Approved) |
| 4 | Train **AI Agent** + test in sandbox |
| 5 | Invite front desk — **Chat-only** |
| 6 | Dry-run reminder to internal test number |
| 7 | Go live with after-hours bot **Active** |

---

## Results clinics care about

- Fewer phone hold times for routine logistics
- Lower no-show rate from labeled reminder broadcasts
- After-hours coverage without hiring night shift

---

## Copy-paste playbook

1. WhatsApp **Support only** — **Settings → Channels**  
2. Labels: `Booking`, `Results`, `Insurance` + location tags  
3. **Receptionist** AI Agent — logistics knowledge only, **Paused** during desk hours  
4. Reminder templates → wait for **Approved**  
5. **Growth** for Sunday **Broadcast** to `Appt-*` labels  
6. **Member (Chat-only)** for reception; manager **Admin**  
7. **Analytics** Monday review
