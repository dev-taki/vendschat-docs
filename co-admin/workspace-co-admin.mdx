## Open Co-Admin in 3 steps

1. Sign in to Vendschat.  
2. Confirm your **workspace** in **Settings** (workspace switcher).  
3. Left sidebar → **AI Workspace Co-Admin** (`/co-admin`).

Empty state headline: **“Just ask”** — *Ask a question about your workspace, inbox, campaigns, or channels.*

---

## What Co-Admin does

| ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
|--------|------|
| Answers **how-to** from Vendschat guides | Message customers |
| Shows **live workspace** counts & lists | Change settings on its own |
| WhatsApp analytics & contact lookup (email/phone) | Read private message **content** in threads |
| Saves **chat history** per user | Access other workspaces |
| Renders **charts** for trends | Train customer AI knowledge bases |

**Setup**  
- How do I connect WhatsApp?  
- How do labels work?

**Live data**  
- Give me a workspace overview

**Strategy**  
- When should I use broadcasts vs templates?  
- Should I use an AI Agent or Generate Reply?

## Co-Admin vs customer AI Agents

| | **Co-Admin** | **AI Agents** |
|--|--------------|---------------|
| Audience | Your employees | Your customers |
| Location | **AI Workspace Co-Admin** | **AI Agents** + **Chat** |
| Data | Product docs + workspace metrics | Your FAQ & website |

Deep comparison: [Co-Admin vs customer AI Agents](/co-admin/co-admin-vs-customer-ai-agents).

## Privacy

Scoped to **your current workspace** and your permissions. Use **Chat** for reading customer messages — use Co-Admin for operating the platform.

---

## Tip for managers

Ask for an overview at the start of team meetings. Compare with **Analytics** for last week’s campaign performance — live snapshot vs historical trends.
