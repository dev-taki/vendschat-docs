Extended Q&A for **AI Workspace Co-Admin** about **Chat**, threads, filters, assignments, composer, and labels.

---

## Basics

### Where is the inbox?

**Chat** in the sidebar → `/chat`. Default landing page after sign-in for most users.

### What is a thread?

One conversation with a customer on a channel (or linked across channels for the same contact). Shows in the left **thread list**.

### Why is my thread list empty?

- No customer has messaged your **connected channels** yet  
- Wrong **workspace** — switch in **Settings**  
- **Channel filter** set to an account with no threads  
- Active filters (**Unread** + label) exclude everything  

Connect channels: [channels docs](/channels/workspaces-and-channels-overview).

### Can multiple agents reply to the same conversation?

**Yes.** All see the same timeline. Use **assignment** and **labels** for ownership clarity.

## Navigation & layout

### What are the three parts of Chat on desktop?

| Column | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Left | Thread list (search, filters) |
| Center | Messages + composer |
| Right | Recipient panel (contact, assign, labels) |

### How does mobile Chat work?

Three screens: **Threads** → tap row → **Conversation** → contact icon → **Recipient**. Back button returns.

### How do I hide the recipient panel?

Desktop: click the small **chevron** on the panel’s left edge.

### What is the empty center logo?

No thread selected. Pick a conversation from the left list.

## Thread list filters

### How do I see only unread messages?

Above thread list: toggle **Unread** (between **All** and **Read**).

### How do I filter by WhatsApp only?

Header **channel dropdown** → select your WhatsApp account (not **All channels**).

### How do I search for a customer?

**Search by Phone or Name…** field — matches contact phone and display name.

### How do I filter by label?

**Funnel** icon → select label(s). **All labels** clears.

### What does the inbound red arrow mean?

Last message was **from the customer** (inbound). Unread threads emphasize this.

## Opening threads & URLs

### How do I share a link to a conversation?

With thread open, copy URL — includes `?thread={id}` e.g. `/chat?thread=42`.

### What is `?dl=` in the URL?

**Deep link token** — opens a specific message (highlighted). Used in notifications or internal links.

### Does opening a thread mark it read?

**Yes**, automatically. Unread count clears for that conversation.

## Replying & composer

### How do I send a message?

1. Open thread  
2. Type in *“Type your message…”*  
3. **Send** or press **Enter** (Shift+Enter for new line)  

### Why can’t I type — only templates?

**24-hour messaging window** expired (WhatsApp/Meta rule). Customer must reply **or** you send an approved **Template**.

Composer shows an expiry banner. Click **Templates**.

### How do I send a WhatsApp template from Chat?

**Templates** button in composer → choose template → fill fields → send.

### How do I pick which channel to send from?

Use **channel dropdown** in composer when contact has WhatsApp + Instagram, etc. Defaults to last **inbound** channel.

### What do delivery ticks mean?

On outbound messages: clock (pending) → single check (sent) → double check (delivered) → blue double check (read). WhatsApp-style.

## Labels

### Where do I create labels?

**Settings → Workspace → Labels** or **Chat** header **Labels** icon.

### How do I tag a conversation?

Conversation **header** label control or **recipient panel** → Labels section.

### Can I auto-tag by keyword?

**Keyword hints** on labels (when configured in Settings) can auto-apply when customers use certain words.

### I only see some labels / threads

You may have **label-scoped access** — ask admin to adjust membership.

---

## Assignments

### How do I assign a thread to someone?

**Recipient panel** → **Assigned to** → pick teammate, **group**, or **AI Agent**.

### What is Unassigned?

Thread in shared pool — no named owner.

### Where do I create groups for routing?

**Settings → Workspace → Groups**.

### How do I invite agents who only reply in Chat?

**Team Members** → Invite as **Member** (Chat-only).

---

## Resolve & status

### How do I close a conversation?

**Recipient panel** → **End Chat** → optional note → confirm. Thread becomes **resolved**.

### Will history disappear?

**No.** Reopening when customer returns keeps prior messages.

### What’s the difference between resolved and unread?

**Resolved** = workflow status (closed). **Unread** = new messages since someone last read.

---

## New Chat & outbound

### How do I message a new customer?

Thread list header → **New Chat** (+) → template modal → channel + phone + template.

Requires active **WhatsApp** for template outbound.

---

## Generate Reply & AI

### Where is Generate Reply?

Composer toolbar → sparkle / **Generate Reply**.

### Does Generate Reply send automatically?

**No.** Human must click **Send**.

### What’s the difference between Generate Reply and AI Agents?

| Generate Reply | AI Agents |
|----------------|-----------|
| Helps **agents** draft | Talks **to customers** |
| Human sends | Can auto-send when assigned |

### What is Co-Admin?

Internal assistant for **your team** — setup help, workspace status. Not customer-facing.

---

## Real-time & notifications

### Do messages appear without refresh?

**Yes** for WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram — live updates in open **Chat**.

### Why is sidebar Chat badge still showing?

May reflect other unread threads. Open **Unread** filter to triage.

---

## Permissions

### I don’t see Chat in the sidebar

Role may lack **Chat** page access — ask owner/admin.

### Can members connect channels?

Usually **no** — **Admin**/**Owner** use **Settings → Channels**.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Try |
|---------|-----|
| Send failed | Check channel **active**; WhatsApp registration modal |
| Wrong customer name | Edit in recipient panel or inline in header |
| Panel won’t load | Reselect thread; refresh page |
| Stale thread list | Scroll to refresh; change filter and back |
| Template list empty | Create templates in **Templates** page; sync WhatsApp |
